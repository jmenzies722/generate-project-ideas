# Consumer lenses

Pick **one lens per idea**. Do not generate five "AI copilots."

## 1. Teach with your hands

Learning by doing in a spatial or interactive surface — not a course
player. The product is the practice loop. Think CubeCoach, not Coursera.

Good seeds: physical skills, visual systems, instruments, crafts,
games with real rules, city navigation, taste training.

## 2. Invisible daily infrastructure

A small tool that removes a repeated friction people currently solve
with a notes app, a group chat, or a spreadsheet. The delight is
speed and taste, not features.

Good seeds: coordination among 2–6 people, neighborhood logistics,
"I always forget this at the wrong moment."

## 3. Local-first / privacy with a face

The data stays on the device (or the home server) and the UI is
beautiful enough that privacy is not the only pitch.

Good seeds: journals, health-adjacent logs, family archives,
homelab-adjacent consumer apps.

## 4. Multiplayer utility

Not a social network. A shared object two or more people edit or
play that would be worse alone. The unit of value is the session.

Good seeds: planning, teaching, collecting, local events, co-op
tools for friends who already have a group chat.

## 5. Agent as a character, not a chat box

An agent that *does a job in the world* with a visible state, not a
textarea. The user watches work happen and can interrupt it.

Good seeds: research that produces an artifact, errands, monitoring
something the user cares about, coaching with receipts.

## 6. Physical × digital

Camera, sensors, 3D, maps, or a real object. The screen is a
viewport, not the whole product.

Good seeds: spatial how-tos, home, sport, music, cities, collections.

## 7. Taste and craft

Opinionated software for people who care how something is made.
Narrow audience, high love. The product has a point of view.

Good seeds: writing, design, cooking, film, training, collecting.

## 8. Time-bound ritual

A product that is used in a specific window (morning, commute,
Sunday reset, trip, season) and is allowed to be silent otherwise.

Good seeds: trip packing, race week, move-in, on-call week for
humans who are not SREs, family Sunday.

## How to twist a lens

Combine a lens with a *scene* (NYC apartment, rec-league team,
new parent, solo founder, homelab nerd) and a *constraint* (works
offline, 10-second interaction, no account, runs on a phone).
The scene + constraint is usually the product.
